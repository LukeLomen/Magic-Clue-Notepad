This is not a game of Clue vs AI. Magic Clue Notepad is a replacement for the paper notepad while playing the board game with family and friends (except you want to make it not fun by looking at a screen and winning every time). Magic Clue Notepad draws itself automatically, you simply record what happens.


Clue Strategy -
Target the last player
Focus on the category with the fewest known/extra cards (often rooms)
Avoid consistency when suggesting
-Narrowing down categories by suggesting on of your cards or an extra card or the hidden card is a good strategy but opponents will catch on (especially if they know the card in your hand that you are using as bait)
