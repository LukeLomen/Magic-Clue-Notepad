Magic Clue Notepad

This is not a game of Clue vs AI. Magic Clue Notepad is a replacement for the paper notepad while playing the board game with family and friends—it handles the bookkeeping and the deduction for you. Enter what happens on each turn and it fills in every player's row automatically, including everything that can be worked out by process of elimination.

How to Play

  • Setup: Enter player names in clockwise seating order, then tap your own starting cards on the table. If the cards don't divide evenly among players, you'll also pick the leftover "extra" cards dealt face up.
  • Making a suggestion: Tap a row to pick who's suggesting, then tap one suspect, one weapon, and one room. Tap the response the suggestee gave—the specific card if you can see it, Unknown if they showed someone else a card you couldn't see, or None if they have none of the three. A "None" automatically passes the same suggestion to the next player in the circle.
  • Browsing history: The ‹/› arrows step back and forward through past turns, showing the table exactly as it looked at that point. On a past turn, pick a different response and press Edit to correct a mistake—everything after it is automatically recomputed.
  • The corner menu (☰): New Game resets everything; the card list switches between real and themed Clue editions; Undo removes the most recent turn to redo it from scratch; and Manual Entry (below) records a card you already know without going through a suggestion.

Manual Entry

Sometimes you already know a card is in someone's hand—you saw it dealt, someone told you outside the game, or you're just confident from a real Clue accusation. Manual Entry lets you tap the player and the card directly, without a suggestion, and it's undone the same way as any other turn.

Table Symbols

  • X — this player has this card
  • / — this player does not have this card
  • ? — this player showed someone one of a few possible cards, not yet narrowed down to one
  • | — an extra card, dealt face up rather than into anyone's hand
  • $ — the hidden card—nobody's hand; it's the answer, in the envelope

Clue Strategy

  • Target the last player in the round.
  • Focus on the category with the fewest known/extra cards (often rooms).
  • Avoid consistency when suggesting—reusing the same "bait" card lets opponents catch on, especially if they know it's in your hand.
